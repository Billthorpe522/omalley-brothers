Our story.

Three generations of family-owned leadership. A fourth taking the wheel. Ninety-four years of doing one thing — and doing it well.

1931 · FOUNDED

The O'Malley brothers open shop.

Two brothers. One truck. A trade learned in the field, not the classroom. They start with residential and small commercial work across Chicago's North Shore.

THREE GENERATIONS

A family trade, passed down.

Jack Reddington carries the work forward into the modern commercial era — high-rises, hospitals, central plants. The company earns its place on the GC bid lists.

TODAY · 4TH GEN

Twin brothers take the wheel.

John and James Reddington — co-vice presidents, fourth generation — are stepping up to lead the company into its next chapter. Same crew. Same standard. New tools.
